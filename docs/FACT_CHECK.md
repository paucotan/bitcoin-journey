## Priority fixes (material errors)

**Claim 4 — M2 70x: WRONG**
- Current on page: "money supply up 70x"
- Correct: ~32–33x (FRED M2SL: $685.5B in Aug 1971 → ~$22,442B in Jan 2026)
- Fix: change to "over 30x" or "roughly 32x"
- Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/M2SL

**Claim 5 — 91% purchasing power loss: WRONG**
- Current on page: "dollar lost 91% of its purchasing power"
- Correct: ~87.6% (consistent with claim 1 which correctly says 87%)
- Note: claims 1 and 5 are currently inconsistent with each other on the page
- Fix: change to ~88% everywhere; align both claims
- Source: https://www.in2013dollars.com/us/inflation/1971?amount=1

**Claim 19 — 6x faster home values vs wages: WRONG**
- Current on page: "Home values rose 6x faster than wages between 1965 and 2021"
- Correct: ~7.6x faster (nominal); in real terms, home prices +118% vs income +15%
- Fix: change to "7–8x faster" or reframe in real terms
- Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/13/how-much-more-expensive-homes-are-since-1965.html

## Secondary fixes (nuance/outdated)

**Claim 2 — 16x house price increase**
- Defensible but at the upper bound; exact multiple depends on which quarter used
- Add "in nominal terms" for precision

**Claim 3 — $25,000 → $400,000**
- Both figures directionally accurate (1971 median ~$24,300–$25,800; current ~$405,300)
- Will need updating as prices change; consider rounding to $410,000

**Claim 7 — M2 grew ~40% between 2020–2022**
- Accurate as a cumulative total (Feb 2020–Apr 2022 peak = ~41%)
- Misleading if read as an annual rate; clarify "in total" or "over the period"

**Claim 13 — 1.4 billion unbanked**
- World Bank Findex 2025 now reports 1.3 billion
- Fix: update to 1.3 billion

**Claim 14 — Remittance fees 6–10%**
- Global average Q3 2024: ~6.2% blended; digital ~4.95%; bank-to-bank up to 13%
- "10%" overstates the typical fee; update to "5–7%" or "averaging around 6%"
- Source: https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/

**Claim 15 — Fed $16 trillion in 2008**
- The $16T figure is from the 2011 GAO audit (Dodd-Frank mandated)
- It is a cumulative flow total, not simultaneous exposure — peak outstanding was ~$1.16T
- The disclosure fact is accurate; the $16T framing needs context or should be removed
- Consider: "The Fed's emergency lending programs were not publicly disclosed until Congress mandated an audit in 2011."

**Claim 20 — productivity/wages diverged in early 1970s**
- EPI data anchors the divergence to 1973, with acceleration post-1979
- "Early 1970s" is defensible; "since 1973" would be more precise
