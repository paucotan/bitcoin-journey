# Creative Brief: Bitcoin Journey

## The Brief

Bitcoin Journey is an educational museum for people who have never been taught monetary history — millennials and Gen Z readers in English-speaking countries who feel economically squeezed but have never been given the language to understand why. The site's founding argument is that economics has been made deliberately inaccessible to ordinary people, and that this opacity is not accidental: it protects the system from scrutiny. The site exists as the antidote to that opacity. Its job is to make the invisible visible — to show, plainly and honestly, how monetary debasement works, who benefits, and what has changed since 1971 — and to do so in a way that leaves the reader not just awake and informed, but genuinely hopeful. The emotional arc the site must deliver is: awake → understand → hopeful → agency. Intuitive analogies (beer, wine, cathedrals) are not decorative — they are the primary editorial method, the mechanism by which a deliberately obscured subject becomes comprehensible to someone encountering it for the first time. The tone throughout is that of a trusted, curious guide: morally serious but never hectoring, evidence-based rather than ideological, and always oriented toward the question "what can you actually do?" The guides must carry the same narrative urgency as the landing page; procedural content must be grounded in human stakes before it earns the right to be technical.
